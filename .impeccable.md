# Design Context — ECOclean Cymru

Design context for AI-assisted design work on this site. Derived from the
current codebase (Oct 2026); items marked *(inferred)* should be confirmed
by the owner.

## Users

- **Homeowners and landlords in Pembrokeshire**: regular cleans, deep cleans,
  end-of-tenancy, holiday-let changeovers. Often browsing on a phone and
  wanting to know quickly: *do they cover my area, are they trustworthy, how
  much, how do I book?*
- **Local businesses**: offices, retail, pubs, restaurants, childcare and
  community venues looking for contract cleaning with flexible hours.
- **Builders and developers**: site welfare and canteen cleaning, plus
  post-construction and handover cleans. They care about reliability,
  accreditation (DBS, CSCS) and turning up on schedule.
- **Job to be done:** get a quote or start a WhatsApp conversation with as
  little friction as possible. Contact goes through the `/contact` form
  (Web3Forms), WhatsApp, the floating quote button and the `/pricing`
  questionnaire.

## Brand Personality

**Three words:** grounded, trustworthy, quietly premium.

- Local and personal ("based here, serving here — not a franchise").
  It's a small Welsh business, not a faceless corporate.
- Eco-conscious without being preachy or "greenwashed".
- Plain-spoken, confident copy: "done properly", "We Clean – Properly".
- Honest: the site deliberately shows "Why choose us" instead of unverified
  reviews, and says "working towards CSCS" rather than overclaiming.
- Emotional goal: visitors should feel reassured and that they're in safe
  hands. The site should read as more polished than the typical local
  cleaner's site, but never cold.

## Aesthetic Direction

**Editorial-organic.** Deep forest greens, warm gold accents, a refined serif
display face, and full-bleed real photography.

- **Colour (HSL tokens in `src/index.css`):**
  - `--eco-primary` 155 45% 18% (forest green, primary actions)
  - `--eco-secondary` 152 40% 32% (hover / secondary)
  - `--eco-dark` 155 100% 10% (dark bands, overlays)
  - `--eco-gold` 42 45% 60% (accent: italics, eyebrows, rules)
  - `--eco-tint` 152 35% 95% (soft section backgrounds)
  - Neutrals use a warm, gold-tinted hue (42), not grey.
  - `theme-color` is `#00683E`.
- **Typography:**
  - Display: *Fraunces* (light weight, italic for emphasis words in gold) via
    `.font-display`
  - Headings: *Outfit*
  - Body: *Inter*
  - Eyebrows: uppercase, wide tracking (`tracking-[0.3em]`), small size,
    gold or muted colour, often led by a thin gold rule.
- **Components:** shadcn/ui primitives. CTAs use the `pill` /
  `pillOutline` button variants (rounded-full, uppercase, tracked). WhatsApp
  uses its own brand green (`whatsapp` variant), the one sanctioned
  off-palette colour.
- **Imagery:** real job photos (before/after pairs in
  `src/lib/beforeAfterGallery`), with a green gradient overlay on the hero.
  Prefer genuine work photos over stock. Credit any stock images in code
  comments.
- **Depth and motion:** soft green-tinted shadows (`--shadow-card`), subtle
  grain overlay, and Framer Motion fade-up on scroll (0.6s, easeOut, once).
  Restrained, never bouncy.
- **Theme:** light-first. Dark tokens exist under `.dark` but are not a
  primary target. *(inferred)*

**Anti-references:** generic blue-and-white "sparkle" cleaning templates,
cartoon bubbles and mascots, clip-art leaves, stock photos of
over-smiling cleaners, aggressive discount banners, and the usual AI tells
(purple gradients, glassmorphism, identical icon-card grids everywhere).

## Design Principles

1. **Trust before polish.** Accreditation, locality and real photos earn the
   enquiry. Never fake reviews, badges or claims.
2. **One obvious next step.** Every section should make "Get a Quote" or
   "WhatsApp Us" easy to reach, especially on mobile.
3. **Warm restraint.** Green and gold on warm whites, generous whitespace,
   light serif display. Use the gold accent sparingly so it stays special.
4. **Local voice.** Write like a Pembrokeshire business owner talking to a
   neighbour: clear, specific, no corporate filler.
5. **Accessible by default.** WCAG AA contrast (watch gold-on-white and
   white/70 text on imagery), real alt text, visible focus states, and
   respect for `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Known Inconsistencies to Resolve

- `index.html` meta and OG descriptions claim "CSCS qualified" / "CSCS card
  holders", but on-page copy says "Working towards CSCS". These should match.
- OG/Twitter images still point at the Lovable placeholder image.
- `README.md` is still the Lovable boilerplate.
